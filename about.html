📋 About Gitanjali Green Port City

Spanning 100 acres of meticulously planned land, Gitanjali Green Port City is our flagship mega venture strategically positioned near the upcoming Ramayapatnam Port, BPCL refinery, Indosol Solar Park, and other multi-crore industrial projects worth over ₹4 lakh crore combined investment.

This venture features:

  • Plot Sizes: Ranging from 150 sq yards to 500+ sq yards
  • Road Infrastructure: 100 ft, 60 ft, and 40 ft wide black-topped roads with street lighting
  • Amenities: Clubhouse, swimming pool, kalyana mandapam, open theater, children's parks, solar street lights, 24/7 security
  • Green Spaces: Over 20% dedicated to parks, plantation, and recreational zones
  • Water Supply: Bore wells, overhead tanks, and underground water taps to every plot
  • Electricity: Underground cabling with dedicated transformers
  • Compound Wall: Fully fenced perimeter with controlled entry/exit gates
